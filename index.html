Advanced coverage options

Round Map

All placements, upgrades, sells, bank actions, absorbs, ability uses, paragon buys, and pop-income timings become actions here.

Action tick is 0 to 1260. End-of-round cash, like 100 + round #, is added after the round. Add optional income tick arrays such as [340,500,560] to release round pop income during the round, or use End + to add custom end-round income.
